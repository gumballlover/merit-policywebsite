Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 13, 2026 • Effective Date: August 13, 2026

Welcome to Merit ("the App", "we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have complete transparency regarding how your data is accessed, processed, and protected. This Privacy Policy governs your use of the Merit mobile application and related services.

1. OVERVIEW & LOCAL-FIRST PHILOSOPHY

Merit is designed with a privacy-first, client-side architecture. The core functionality of Merit—tracking your screen time, blocking apps, counting workout repetitions via computer vision, calculating earned time credits, and logging habits—operates entirely on your local device. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or transmit your personal screen time history or exercise camera feeds to third-party advertisers or remote analytical brokers.

2. DATA WE ACCESS, PROCESS, AND STORE

2.1 Usage Statistics (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS)

  • What We Access: The App queries the Android UsageStatsManager and UsageEvents to determine the amount of time spent in active foreground applications.
  • Purpose: To calculate your daily screen time, enforce your custom time limits and time banks, display usage statistics, and determine when an application should be locked.
  • Storage & Transmission: All usage calculations and historical app statistics are processed and cached strictly locally on your device. They are never uploaded to any cloud server.

2.2 Device Administrator Permission (BIND_DEVICE_ADMIN)

  • What We Access: When you optionally enable Strict Mode, Merit requests Device Administrator status (TimeBankDeviceAdminReceiver).
  • Purpose: This permission is used exclusively to prevent accidental or compulsive uninstallation of the App while you are in a locked focus session.
  • Control: You retain full control to disable Device Administrator access at any time through the Merit Settings screen or Android System Settings.

2.3 Camera & On-Device AI Pose Detection (CAMERA)

  • What We Access: Real-time camera video stream during user-initiated physical workout tasks (Planks).
  • Purpose: To analyze physical body joints (skeletal landmarks) via on-device Google ML Kit Pose Detection in order to accurately assess form and hold duration.
  • Zero Video Recording / Transmission: Merit does not record, take snapshots, store, or transmit any video footage or camera images. Every video frame is analyzed instantly in memory on your physical hardware and immediately discarded.

2.4 Location Data (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION)

  • What We Access: Approximate and precise geographic coordinates when you engage in Location Lock tasks (e.g. visiting a gym, library, or park).
  • Purpose: To verify that you have arrived at your designated destination to earn focus credits.
  • Storage: Merit does not track your ongoing location history in the background for surveillance or commercial profiling.

2.5 Physical Activity & Health Connect (ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION, health.READ_STEPS)

  • What We Access: Step counts and walking/running distances through Android Activity Recognition, device pedometers, and optional Google Health Connect sync.
  • Purpose: To calculate earned screen time based on your physical steps and movement.
  • Storage: Step totals are stored locally in the App's secure preferences.

2.6 Authentication & Account Data (Firebase Auth & Google Sign-In)

  • What We Access: Your Google account name, email address, unique Firebase User ID (UID), and profile picture URL.
  • Purpose: To authenticate your account, secure your identity, and display your chosen username and avatar on your personal profile and stats.
  • Security: Handled directly by Google Firebase Authentication in compliance with industry-standard TLS encryption.

3. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES & INTEGRATIONS

Merit integrates with trusted third-party providers for specific functions:

  • Google Firebase Authentication: Manages secure sign-in and user authentication (Google Privacy Policy).
  • Google ML Kit: Performs on-device machine learning pose detection (Google ML Kit Terms).
  • OpenStreetMap & FlutterMap: Renders map tiles during location-based tasks.

4. DATA RETENTION & SECURITY

  • Local Storage: All app preferences, time banks, credits, and streak history are stored in Android secure app-private storage (SharedPreferences).
  • Security: We apply strict technical and organizational safeguards against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration.
  • Data Deletion: You can delete your local data at any time by clearing the application storage in Android Settings or using the Sign Out option in Settings.

5. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY (COPPA & GDPR)

Merit is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under these ages. If we become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, we will delete it promptly.

6. YOUR RIGHTS (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request deletion or correction of your personal data.
  • Revoke previously granted permissions (Usage Stats, Camera, Location) at any time via Android System Settings.
